| **App activity / Device IDs** | **No** — the app requests no notification permission and stores no advertising id |
| Location, Contacts, Photos, Files, Financial info, Health, Messages, Calendar | **No** — none of it, and the app asks for no such permission |
| Is all data encrypted in transit? | **Yes** (HTTPS throughout) |
| Can users request data deletion? | **Yes** — the address in the privacy policy |

**"Shared" means handed to another company for their own use.** Your database host, SMS provider and the
app's build service process data *on your behalf*, which Google's form explicitly excludes from "shared" —
so the honest answer there is no.

## Screenshots — the one thing I cannot make for you

Play requires **at least 2** phone screenshots, 16:9 or 9:16, between 320px and 3840px on each side.

Take them on the phone while the app is running:

1. The home screen with the hero and the figures
2. عروضنا, the catalogue
3. An offer with its photographs
4. احسب مشروعك mid-calculation
5. فضاء «زيتونتي» signed in

Four or five is better than two — the listing shows them as a carousel, and an app with two reads as unfinished.

## The category, and why it is a real decision

**Finance** is the honest fit: people buy an asset and pay in instalments. It is also the category Google
scrutinises hardest, and some financial categories can only be published by organisation accounts.

**Lifestyle** or **Shopping** are defensible — the app itself takes no money, offers no credit and gives no
investment advice; it is a catalogue, a calculator and a customer file. That is exactly the reasoning behind
answering **"None of the above"** on the app-categories declaration during signup.

Pick one and keep the two answers consistent. If the declaration said "no financial products", the category
should not say Finance.
